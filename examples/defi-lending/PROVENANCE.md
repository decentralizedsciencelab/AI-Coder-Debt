## What was copied, and what was not

The copy is byte-identical to `extracted/`. No files were added, removed, or
edited.

**The extracted tree is a subset of what the model actually emitted.** The raw
response contains a `hardhat.config.js` and a `scripts/deploy.js`, both printed
in full. Neither appears in `extracted_files`, so neither is here. The response
also places the contracts under `contracts/`; the extracted tree has them flat at
the root.

This is load-bearing for the DRS result. Both `build_config_exists` and
`deploy_script_exists` report FAIL on this tree, and both would plausibly pass
had extraction captured the two files the model did write. Neither failure
affects the score, because both are marked not-applicable — see `ANALYSIS.md`.
